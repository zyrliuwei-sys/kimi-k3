## Copy a structure, not a finished idea

A strong prompt gives the model a subject, an art direction, a composition, and an intended frame. The six examples below are starting structures. Replace the objects, colors, and audience details so the result belongs to your project.

## 1. Product launch still life

```text
Premium matte-black wireless headphones on a sculptural white pedestal, soft studio lighting, subtle shadows, editorial product photography, clean beige backdrop, 4:5 vertical composition
```

Use this for physical products. Swap the object, material, backdrop, and frame; keep the lighting direction specific.

## 2. Travel poster

```text
Vintage travel poster for Kyoto in spring, cherry blossoms framing a quiet street, screen-print texture, coral and indigo palette, bold cream typography space at top, 2:3 poster layout
```

Choose a location with a distinctive season, landmark, or palette. Mention the required empty area if copy will be added later.

## 3. Editorial portrait

```text
Fashion editorial portrait of a person in a tailored cobalt coat, daylight through frosted glass, restrained expression, fine film grain, muted gray background, magazine photography, 3:4
```

For people, describe wardrobe, emotional tone, light, and framing instead of adding unrelated style words.

## 4. Architectural concept

```text
Compact cliffside reading room built from warm timber and glass, misty coastal morning, dramatic cantilever, architectural visualization, natural materials, wide 16:9 view
```

Architecture responds well to a building type, materials, site, time of day, and camera framing.

## 5. App campaign image

```text
A cheerful runner holding a smartphone after a morning workout, sunlit city park, bright athletic campaign art direction, breathable negative space on the left for headline, 4:3
```

State where the headline or interface overlay belongs so the image has usable composition, not just an attractive subject.

## 6. Fantasy book cover

```text
An ancient observatory floating above a sea of clouds at twilight, a lone astronomer on the terrace, luminous constellations, cinematic fantasy illustration, deep navy and gold, 2:3 book-cover composition
```

For illustrative work, pair a specific setting with one human-scale detail. It gives the scene a story without overloading the frame.

## Make the second pass smaller

After the first image, change one visible decision: light, crop, point of view, palette, or material. Keeping the rest of the prompt stable gives you a reliable review loop and makes good results easier to repeat.
